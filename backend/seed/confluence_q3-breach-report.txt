# Q3 Security Incident Report

## Classification: CONFIDENTIAL

## Summary
On 2026-08-15, an unauthorized actor gained access to the staging database via a leaked service account credential found in a public Slack channel.

## Impact
- ~2,000 staging customer records exposed.
- Service account staging-deploy compromised.

## Root Cause
Credential committed to a public Slack channel by a contractor during deployment troubleshooting.

## Remediation
1. Rotated all staging service account credentials.
2. Implemented secret scanning on Slack messages.
3. Restricted CI/CD secret access to senior engineers.
