Issue: PAY-456 | Project: Payment Service (PAY)
Type: Bug | Status: Closed | Priority: P0

On 2026-09-14, the payment service experienced a 45-minute outage affecting all Singapore transactions.

Root Cause:
Cascading failure triggered by Redis failover. The circuit breaker on the payment-gateway client was disabled during a previous deployment, allowing the failure to cascade.

Follow-up:
- Created PAY-460: Re-enable circuit breaker.
- Created PAY-461: Add circuit breaker config validation to CI.
