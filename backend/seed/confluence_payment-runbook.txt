# Payment Service Incident Runbook

## Last updated: 2026-09-27 13:00 SGT

## Triage Steps
1. Check Stripe webhook health dashboard.
2. Verify payment-service pod health.
3. Check Redis connectivity for idempotency keys.

## Failover Procedure
1. Switch payment-service to backup region.
2. **NEW STEP (added 2026-09-27):** Enable circuit breaker on payment-gateway client with 50% threshold before failover to prevent cascade.
3. Drain connections from primary region.
4. Verify failover via health check endpoint.

## Escalation
- P0: Page on-call + VP Engineering
- P1: Page on-call only
